Simulation Interfaces & Grading Guide

A visual reference illustrating candidate workspaces, task types, dynamic role determination, and how every submission is accurately graded.

1. Role & Sub-Role Determination

Sub-roles in Gainday are not fixed categories. They are flexible sub-domains dynamically inferred from the job description's responsibilities, tools, and outputs.

Customer Service (Sample Observed Sub-Roles)

Tier 1 Support (Email & Chat) • Complaints & Escalations • Technical Support • Contact Centre • Support Lead & QA • Retention

Finance (Sample Observed Sub-Roles)

Reconciliation • Financial Reporting • Accounts Payable / Receivable • Budgeting & Forecasting (FP&A) • Audit & Treasury • Private Equity

Sales (Sample Observed Sub-Roles)

SDR / BDR (Outbound Prospecting) • Account Executive (Closing & Negotiations) • Enterprise / Account Management • Sales Operations / Inbound

Cross-Functional / Generic Roles

Any domain outside the above (e.g., Logistics, Operations, People/HR, Product). The system extracts the posting's own 2–4 word domain name.
How Determination Works: The AI classifier evaluates actual day-to-day tools and deliverables (e.g. Zendesk, Excel financial modeling, Salesforce pipeline) rather than relying on job titles. The employer's top-level role choice is respected, while the dynamic sub-role refines candidate task selection and scopes anti-repetition checks.

⚙️ Objective Machine Grading

📝 Open-Ended Rubric Grading

Scored across 4 criteria (Judgment, Written Communication, Problem Solving, Domain Awareness) against 5-point anchored rubrics. Hard Safety Gate: Violating policy or making unauthorized refund commitments triggers an auto-fail cap.
